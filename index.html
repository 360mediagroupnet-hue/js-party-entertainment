07Good to know

Questions, answered.

How big is the Rave Cave?

It is 6 metres wide, 8 metres long and 4.3 metres high, and it fits up to 50 guests.

What comes with it?

The inflatable nightclub, air con, 2 JBL Bluetooth speakers, a smoke machine and lights. You can add DJ J and LED furniture.

What if it rains or it is a hot night?

The Rave Cave is weather-proof and air conditioned.

Can it go inside?

Yes. It works for indoor and outdoor events.

How long does set up take?

Allow at least 3 hours before guests arrive. Set up is included in the price.

Can the party go late?

Yes. Parties that run past 10pm need the overnight hire.

Where do you travel?

Caloundra to Noosa and the surrounding areas.
